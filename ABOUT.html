A Moment in Time

Café 9:04 offers a seasonally inspired menu highlighting local ingredients and thoughtful cooking. Our cozy space in the heart of downtown welcomes guests for breakfast, lunch, and dinner with a changing menu that reflects our commitment to quality and community.

Since opening in 2023, we've focused on creating a vibrant gathering place where hospitality and handcrafted food come together. Whether you're stopping by for morning coffee, a leisurely weekend brunch, or intimate dinner, we hope you'll feel at home.
