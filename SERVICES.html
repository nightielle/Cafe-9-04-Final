RESERVATION

Reserve Your Table

Make your visit to Café 9:04 even more special by reserving your table in advance. We welcome both intimate gatherings and larger group bookings.

Regular Seating

Reserve a table for your regular café visit to ensure your spot even during our busiest hours.

Group Bookings

Planning to come with a larger group? We can arrange special seating for groups of 6 or more people.

Private Events

For special celebrations, we offer the option to reserve a section of our café or even the entire space for your exclusive use.

Make a Reservation

Reservation Policy

  • Please make reservations at least 24 hours in advance.
  • For group bookings (6+ people), please reserve at least 48 hours in advance.
  • We hold reservations for 15 minutes past the scheduled time.
  • For cancellations, please notify us at least 4 hours before your scheduled reservation.
